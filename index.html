Questions?

Quick answers to what people ask most. Anything else, just ask.

Is it free for guests?

Yes. Guests don't need coins. They turn the knob and the sticker is theirs.

How many stickers come in the machine?

Each of the 3 slots holds 100 stickers, so a full machine has 300. Refills are $30 per slot.

What kinds of events is it for?

Weddings, parties, markets, brand activations, festivals, pop-ups and school events. If people gather, stickers work.

What if it breaks?

Text or email us any day of the week. Damage from normal use is covered.

When do you need my artwork?

5 days before your event, as a PNG or PDF. If you need design help, get in touch earlier so we have time to work on it together.
